# Google Play store listing

The text and graphics for RepLiT's main store listing (English), made for the
first closed test (27 Nov 2026). Play Console → Grow users → Store presence →
Main store listing.

## App name (30 characters at most)

RepLiT - Fire Reporting

## Short description (80 characters at most)

Report a fire fast, get alerts for fires near you, and see help on the way.

## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

RepLiT helps you report a fire in seconds and see help on the way.

See a fire? Open RepLiT and tap Report. Your location is filled in for you; add a photo and a short note if you can. Your report goes straight to the fire responders who use RepLiT, so they know where to go. You can follow it from reported to verified, on the way, on scene and fire out.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Report a fire in a few taps, with your location filled in
• Get an alert when a fire is reported near you
• See active fires, fire hydrants and evacuation sites on a live map
• Follow your report until the fire is out
• Call emergency hotlines from the app
• Use it in English or Filipino

WHERE IT WORKS
RepLiT serves Pasay City, Metro Manila, Philippines.

YOUR PRIVACY
RepLiT uses your location to place your report and to alert you about fires nearby. Your data travels over a secure connection and is never sold or used for ads. You can delete your account at any time: in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or at https://reportwhatslit-app.vercel.app/delete-account

In a life-threatening emergency, also call 911.

RepLiT is an independent community project. It is not an official app of any government agency.
